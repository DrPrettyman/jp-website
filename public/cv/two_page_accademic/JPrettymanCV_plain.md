## Academic Background

### Ph.D. Mathematics — University of Reading

Thesis: Tipping Points and Early Warning Signals with Applications to Geophysical Data (http://centaur.reading.ac.uk/98364/1/23022044_Prettyman_Thesis_Joshua%20Prettyman.pdf)

- Thesis develops spectral analysis methods to detect correlations in multidimensional time series, which are used as Early Warning Signals for tipping events in stochastic dynamical systems.
- Methods applied to real geospatial time series data: hurricane paths and greening/desertification.
- Publication of three papers in respected journals:
  - A novel scaling indicator of early warning signals (EPL, 2018) (https://doi.org/10.1209/0295-5075/121/10002)
